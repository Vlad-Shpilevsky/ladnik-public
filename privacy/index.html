Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06. LADNIK is operated by an individual based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. This individual is the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Contact: ladnik.app@gmail.com.

LADNIK organizes tasks, plans, notes, user-entered financial records, contacts and medication schedules in personal and shared Workspaces.

Local profiles and online accounts

Local content is stored on your device. An online account can be connected later; applicable data then synchronizes with the server. For operations subject to current-Terms checks, acceptance is verified against the server version; an unknown status does not authorize that operation. Existing pending operations retain their request identity until safely resolved. Local use does not require online acceptance. Operating-system backups depend on the device and OS. Opening public pages contacts the page-hosting service separately.

Online accounts use email, user ID, name, optional surname, username and authentication/session information. Social sign-in, when configured, is processed through Supabase and the chosen provider; the provider's own processing applies. LADNIK does not claim that a provider configuration has been accepted merely because a login button exists. Optional avatars are stored in Supabase Storage. Do not submit passwords, sign-in codes or session tokens to support.

Content, collaboration and permissions

The service stores the records and attachments you choose to create, including optional financial and health information. Online/shared records are processed according to Workspace membership, role and record visibility. Being mentioned or assigned a task does not itself grant access to a private record. Team membership does not automatically grant Workspace access. Names, authorship and permitted history may be visible to other authorized participants. Choose the audience carefully and have a lawful basis before sharing another person's information.

Data is used for authentication, storage, synchronization, collaboration, permissions, notifications and recovery of reliable operation. Supabase provides Auth, PostgreSQL, applicable Realtime updates and Storage. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption. Device security and backups also depend on your operating system and settings.

We process the account details and content needed to provide the features you request, including synchronization, collaboration and subscription verification. Security, fraud prevention, reliable operation and handling requests are additional purposes. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, these activities rely on performance of the service agreement, applicable legal obligations or, where legally permitted, legitimate interests subject to the required balancing against your rights. Where consent is required, including for particular sensitive information or optional processing, that processing requires valid consent or another applicable legal exception. Accepting the Terms is not by itself consent to every use of personal or health information.

Do not enter another person's sensitive information without the required authority. Making a record visible to Workspace members does not make it public or authorize unrelated uses. You can choose not to use optional medication, AI, voice or Telegram features. A device permission controls the relevant device capability; it does not replace any separate consent required by law.

LADNIK stores account/installation identifiers, notification settings, applicable device registration tokens and delivery/deduplication state to deliver permitted notifications. AI, voice and external processing

Assistant text and a bounded authorized context may be sent to OpenAI for supported AI functions. An available voice feature may temporarily send recorded audio for speech recognition and then process the transcript through the same authorized command flow. LADNIK does not maintain an ordinary raw-audio or unrestricted chat-history archive or guarantee provider Zero Data Retention. Model requests, responses and external-provider retention are subject to that provider's terms. Review AI suggestions before applying them. Created records follow the ordinary rules of the selected Personal/Workspace source.

AI accounting stores numerical usage, reservations, cost and outcome information to enforce separate Personal/Team budgets and the project ceiling. The accounting ledger does not contain prompt/response text or audio. Uncertain outcomes may keep a reservation until reconciled; changing the calendar month does not erase an unresolved operation or its evidence.

Reports, blocking and Terms

Reports contain reporter/target identifiers, bounded context, a reason, an optional explanation and status/timestamps. The target of a shared-content report is derived from canonical ownership. A short permitted title may be included; full Note/Task/Expense bodies are not copied automatically. Reporting a model-generated response uses only the selected bounded response, not the full prompt, conversation, audio or context. Other ordinary users cannot read reports or reporter identity. Reports do not automatically penalize an account. Abuse protection and authorized moderation use bounded operational metadata and audit.

The existing active-account Terms acceptance records the current version and time and cascades on account deletion. LADNIK does not create a separate permanent post-deletion archive of consent, former email, IP address or device proof. Blocking limits applicable future discovery/invitations but does not automatically erase shared records or existing memberships.

Retention, security and deletion

Online production connections use HTTPS. Server ACL and record visibility checks supplement the interface. Do not share credentials. Records are retained for the selected features until removed through the applicable lifecycle; authorized shared records may remain after a participant leaves or deletes their account. Logs, backups, retention periods and legal obligations: Content and account information are kept to provide the selected features until removed through their supported lifecycle. Preserved shared records retain the minimum references needed for their authorized history. Necessary billing references remain while needed to honor paid access, reconcile refunds or revocations, prevent replay or satisfy applicable legal duties; this does not preserve a deleted profile. Support correspondence is kept to handle the request and any related dispute or legal duty. Retention depends on the continuing purpose, applicable duties and provider settings; no universal automatic purge or fixed backup-expiry period is promised. Deleted live data is not a claim of immediate removal from provider logs, retained copies or device backups. Provider records and inaccessible device copies require their separate applicable handling.

Request account deletion at Account deletion without signing in. If in-app deletion is available, use Profile → Delete account; otherwise use the external request route. Owned Workspaces require an explicit supported deletion or transfer decision. Confirmed completion removes Auth identity, identifying profile information, avatar objects, personal settings, device registrations/notifications and active memberships/direct access, with the disclosed exceptions for shared history and necessary billing records. Local cleanup is confirmed only after successful deletion; an interrupted or unknown result is not reported as success. An external request cannot directly clear an inaccessible device or OS backup.

If a shared Workspace is preserved, its records and valid history can remain with Deleted user attribution and the minimum references needed for consistency. User-entered free text is not promised to be automatically anonymized. Deleting the app or LADNIK account does not cancel a Store subscription; manage auto-renewal separately in Google Play. External providers' deletion/retention obligations must be handled separately, not presented as an immediate universal erasure guarantee.
