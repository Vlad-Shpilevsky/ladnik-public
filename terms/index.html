Creating or uploading online/shared content requires explicit acceptance of the current server Terms version. Free, Pro and Team subscriptions

Free provides access within its current limits. Pro is personal; Team applies to a selected Team and does not replace Workspace permissions or give every member personal Pro. Monthly and yearly offers depend on the Store and application. Disabled or unsupported capabilities are not available offers.

Before checkout Google Play shows the localized price, billing period and applicable trial. Trial eligibility is determined by the Store. Review the trial duration, subsequent recurring price and billing start before confirming. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. LADNIK does not invent a fallback price or allow checkout when the Store price is unavailable or ambiguous. Access requires server verification of the subscription and purchaser. Verified Google test and production purchases use the same Pro/Team entitlement, limit and lifecycle mechanism; the test environment remains explicitly identified. A test purchase is not represented as a production charge, and a previous display-only test status or client success alone does not establish access. Apple subscriptions require their own supported and verified integration.

Manage or cancel future renewal in Google Play subscriptions. Ordinary cancellation normally keeps access until the paid-through date; a refund or revocation can end access sooner. Cancellation does not automatically produce a refund. Google Play refund rules and mandatory applicable rights continue to apply. Restore checks an existing purchase and does not require another purchase or authorize access to someone else's subscription.

Current limits are: Free, up to 3 owned cloud Workspaces, including up to 2 shared Workspaces, with up to 3 members including the owner per ordinary shared Workspace; Pro, up to 20 owned cloud Workspaces, including up to 15 shared Workspaces, with up to 5 members including the owner per ordinary shared Workspace; Team, up to 25 attached Workspaces and 50 Team members including the owner. Local Workspaces do not count toward the cloud limit. These are separate limits: Team membership does not give access to every attached Workspace or personal Pro. The current server-verified allowance applies to an operation. A downgrade does not authorize deletion of existing records merely to bring usage under a limit.

Deleting LADNIK or your LADNIK account does not cancel Store billing. You can request account deletion after reviewing that warning and separately manage auto-renewal in the Store. A Team owner must resolve Team ownership or closure without deleting other people's data. Where the qualified transfer feature is available, the former payer cancels renewal, the same Team retains only its verified paid-through period, and the next owner purchases separately. No Google purchase or payment method is transferred. Closing a Team or an expired plan does not itself erase its Workspaces, records or financial history; applicable write limits may restrict further changes while reading/export remain available.
