The responsible party is the LADNIK operator; LADNIK is operated by an individual based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Privacy Policy: Privacy Policy.

In the app

If in-app deletion is available, open Profile → Delete account. If it is not available, use the external request below. For each Workspace you own, choose deletion or transfer to an eligible member, then confirm account deletion. A connection to the server is required. An incomplete or unknown result does not mean success: use the available recovery actions and do not blindly resend the request. Local data is not considered cleared until success is confirmed.

External request

You can request deletion outside the app via ladnik.app@gmail.com. Through this address you can send a request without signing in to LADNIK.

Include your online account email and a request to delete your LADNIK account. Do not send your password, sign-in codes, session tokens or private Workspace content. Account ownership verification, procedure and processing times: Send the request from the address associated with the account. We will verify account ownership in a proportionate way through the established contact; a password, sign-in code and session token are not required for this. We will agree on what happens to the Workspaces and Team you own, then process the request through a verified available process. First response: within 7 days; this is not a promise of completion within 7 days and not a replacement for mandatory legal deadlines. If the result is incomplete, we first clarify the status without blindly repeating the deletion. For Workspaces you own, deletion/transfer will need to be agreed.

Data deletion and retention

Deleting the app or your LADNIK account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Before deletion, check it and, if necessary, cancel it in Google Play subscriptions; cancelling renewal and requesting a refund are separate actions. For a paid Team you own, ownership and billing must be resolved safely; the purchase is not automatically transferred to another account. If this operation is not available, contact ladnik.app@gmail.com without sending purchase tokens or card details. Store/provider payment records are subject to separate retention requirements.

When deletion is confirmed as complete, the following are deleted: the Auth identity, the identifying profile representation, avatar photos in Storage, personal settings/Quick Actions, notifications, Push registrations and delivery states. Active membership, direct permissions and related invitations/access links are removed. The local SQLite is cleared after confirmed in-app deletion. An external request cannot directly erase data on an inaccessible device; local cleanup requires action on the device.

Valid records and history in transferred or otherwise preserved shared Workspaces may remain, because they belong to those Workspaces. Historical attribution becomes Deleted user; necessary links preserve the integrity of the history. Shared free text is not promised to be automatically anonymized. If deletion of a Workspace is chosen, its domain records are deleted under the current contract.

Retention periods depend on the purpose of the data and applicable obligations. Minimal billing references may be retained for confirmed paid access, refunds, revocation and replay protection; this does not retain the deleted user's profile. Completing the deletion of current data does not mean immediate deletion of logs, provider copies or copies on an inaccessible device. Automatic deletion of all copies by a single date is not promised. Detailed grounds and retention criteria: Privacy Policy. Help: Support / ladnik.app@gmail.com.

For a local profile there is no LADNIK online account yet; data remains on the device, and clearing the device/system copies depends on the OS.
