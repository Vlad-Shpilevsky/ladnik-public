A short guide to how LADNIK works and where things are. It describes the latest version of the app. If something here is missing on your screen, update LADNIK on Google Play.

Common questions

What is the task "Condition" for?

The "When to show the task" field (called "Condition" in earlier versions) hides a task until it is time to do it. The default is "Right away": the task shows at once. Other options:

  • "On date": the task appears on the chosen day (you can add a time).
  • "After another task": it appears once the chosen task is done.
  • "After a plan": it appears once the chosen plan is completed.
  • "When I mark it myself": describe what you are waiting for; the task appears when someone picks "Condition met" in its menu.

Under the field LADNIK says in plain words when the task will appear, for example "The task appears in lists on 20.11". Until then the task is waiting: it is not counted on Today and never becomes overdue. Waiting tasks are listed under "Waiting for condition" with the reason: in the tool's list, on the Due today screen and in the My tasks widget.

The field is under "More". Once it is filled in, it always stays in view. If a task seems to have disappeared, open it and check this field.

Why is my task not on the Today screen?

Check these in order:

  1. The task is waiting for a condition. Look under "Waiting for condition" and see the question above.
  2. The task is not assigned to you. Today shows only your tasks: in a workspace, the ones assigned to you; in My LADNIK, your own tasks with no other assignee. Assign an unassigned task in a shared workspace to yourself, or pick "Claim task" in its menu.
  3. It is not due today. The Overdue and Due today counters go by the due date. A task due tomorrow is only in My open. The My tasks widget shows your tasks without a date filter.
  4. The widget does not read that tool. If a widget has a fixed list of tools, new tools are not added to it automatically. The next question explains how to fix that.

How do I change a widget's sources?

  • Tap the widget. If it has several sources, the "Choose a tool" sheet opens. At the bottom there is "Edit widget", and if there are suitable tools the widget does not show yet, a line "More tools available: N · Show here". "Show here" adds them at once; "Undo" puts things back.
  • Use "Set up". On Today, tap "Set up" (or press and hold a widget), select the widget and open "Settings". Under "Tools", choose the tools you want. "All compatible" means the widget reads every suitable tool in all your workspaces, including new ones.

If a widget has a single source, tapping it opens that tool directly. Change its sources through "Set up" instead.

Where do I change the currency?

Each account has its own currency. You choose a default currency when you sign up and can change it later in Profile → "Personal details" → "Default currency". See "Finances and currencies" for more.

Getting started

There are four tabs at the bottom: Today, Workspaces, Assistant and Profile.

On first launch you can sign in or choose "Continue locally". Without an account your data stays on this device only, and My LADNIK, invitations and sync between devices are not available.

A new user answers "What do you do?", chooses "Just me" or "With others" and taps "Start". LADNIK creates a workspace with suitable tools. You can change everything later. To build it yourself, choose "I will set it up myself".

You can take the short tour of the tabs again: Profile → "Learn LADNIK".

Today and widgets

Today brings your day together from all your workspaces:

  • Quick buttons "Task", "Expense", "Note" and "More" (plan, contact, medication). LADNIK remembers where you add records.
  • The Overdue, Due today and My open counters. They count only your open tasks that are not waiting for a condition. Tap one to see the list. The Due today screen has Overdue, Today and Waiting for condition sections.
  • "First steps" for newcomers. You can hide them.
  • "Needs attention": your overdue tasks, tasks due within a day and invitations.
  • "My widgets".

Search and notifications are at the top.

Setting up widgets

Tap "Set up" or press and hold a widget. In this mode you drag widgets around, and the bar at the bottom has "Add", "Compact layout", "Settings", "Remove" and "Done". A screen holds up to 30 widgets.

Widget types: Greeting, My tasks, Calendar, Money, Account, Plans, Recent changes, Notes, Medications, plus Workspaces on Today. A removed widget can be brought back with "Restore".

In a widget's settings you change the card title, the tools, the record limit and "For whom": "All", "Assigned to me", "Assigned by me", "No assignee" or "Waiting for condition · mine".

A widget on Today shows data from all your workspaces, and each row names its workspace. A widget on a workspace dashboard shows only that workspace. Widgets do not copy records: tapping one opens the original tool.

Workspaces and tools

A workspace is a place for one area of life or work: family, a project, a workshop. It has tools, participants and its own widget dashboard. You can use a workspace on your own too.

My LADNIK is the first card on the Workspaces tab. It holds your personal tools, which only you can see, including "Personal finance". My LADNIK needs a linked, verified account.

Create a workspace

  1. On the Workspaces tab, tap "+" → "Create workspace".
  2. Enter a name and, if you like, a description, colour, icon and type.
  3. The "Add tool" gallery opens. Pick a bundle or individual tools and tap "Connect".

The gallery has a "Find a template" search and the filters Home and family, Work and projects, Small business. The preview shows sample records; they are not added to your tool. "Another tool" is an empty tool without a template. Only the workspace owner can connect tools.

Press and hold a card to change the order of workspaces. Only the owner can delete a workspace. It is deleted with all its records and cannot be restored.

Bundles

A bundle adds several tools to a new workspace at once.

  • Home and family: Home chores, Finance, Notes
  • Personal: Tasks, Notes, Finance
  • Projects and team: Projects, Tasks, Notes
  • Services and repairs: Requests, Clients, Finance
  • Client bookings: Appointments, Clients, Finance

Tool templates

  • Tasks — opens as: list
  • Requests — opens as: board; extra statuses: Waiting for client, Cancelled; fields: Client phone, Amount
  • Appointments — opens as: calendar; extra statuses: Cancelled; fields: Client phone, Service
  • Projects — opens as: board
  • Clients — opens as: list; fields: Source
  • Notes — opens as: list
  • Home chores — opens as: list
  • Finance — opens as: list

Tool settings

In "Tool settings" you set what a record is called, the default view (list, board or calendar), statuses and extra fields. Field types: text, number, money, date and time, choice, yes/no. A field can be required: the record is not saved while it is empty. "Show on record card" shows the field in the list; it does not hide it from participants.

The tool menu has "Rename", "Tool settings" and "Delete".

Participants and access

The owner of a workspace saved to an account can invite people. Open the workspace → "Participants" → "Invite participant" and find the person by name or @username (at least three characters). You choose the role when inviting.

  • Owner manages the workspace, its tools and participants.
  • Editor creates and edits records.
  • View only sees records but cannot change them.

The invitation arrives in notifications and in Profile → "Invitations", where it can be accepted or declined. The owner can change a participant's role, revoke an invitation, remove a participant or transfer ownership ("Transfer" or "Transfer and leave"). Team rights and payment do not move with the transfer. A participant can leave a workspace on their own.

A contact in the Contacts tool is just a record. It does not make the person a participant or give them access.

Tasks

Task fields

In view: title, due date ("Today", "Tomorrow", "Date" or "No due date"; after a date you can add a time), Repeat, status and assignee. In a shared workspace the assignee is in the "People" row. Under "More": "When to show the task", "Linked plan", "Contact" and "Description". A field from "More" that is filled in always stays in view.

Assignee

The assignee can be any active participant of the workspace, or "Unassigned". In a personal tool, and in a workspace where you are the only participant, a new task is assigned to you. The assignee gets the notification "A task was assigned to you". Anyone can take an unassigned task with "Claim task".

Due date

A task is overdue once its day has passed or, if it has a time, once that time has passed. Waiting and completed tasks never become overdue.

Due date reminders

Tasks and plans in a shared workspace have a "Reminder" row. You need a workspace saved to your account and the owner or editor role. My LADNIK has no due date reminders yet.

  • New record: tap "Reminder", turn on "Remind me before it's due" and choose "1 day before", "1 hour before" or "Custom…" (in minutes, up to 10080, which is 7 days). You can pick up to two reminders. The reminder is saved together with the record if the due date has a time. The due date and the reminder moment must be in the future, and you need a connection.
  • Saved record: in "Reminder" choose "When to remind" and tap "Save reminder". If the due date has no time, pick the moment in the "Date and time" row. "Disable reminder" removes it.
  • "Repeat every 4 hours" repeats it up to 3 times. Repeats are off by default: if the recipient has not allowed them in "My reminders" (in "Notifications"), a reminder with a repeat is not saved. Turn the repeat off or ask the recipient to allow repeats.

The reminder goes to the record's assignee. Without an assignee the reminder is not saved. It arrives in LADNIK notifications, and as Push or in Telegram if those are on for "Deadlines and reminders". The reminder is not saved and does not arrive if the recipient turned off "Receive reminders", turned off "In the app" for "Deadlines and reminders" or set the workspace to "No notifications". During the recipient's quiet hours it waits until they end, but no longer than a day after the due date. A completed or waiting task and a completed plan get no reminder. If you change the due date, assignee, status or access to the record, the reminder is turned off: set it again.

Statuses and returning a task

The default task statuses are New, In progress and Completed. You can change them in the tool settings. If you uncheck a completed task, LADNIK offers "Return for revision". A comment is optional. Returns are listed in "Return history".

Repeating tasks

In the "Repeat" row choose "Every day", "On weekdays", "On days of the week…", "Every week" or "Every month". Under "End" choose "No end", "Until a date" or "Number of times" (up to 366). The line below shows the result, for example "Every day until 17.12 · next: tomorrow". A repeat needs a due date: if there is none, LADNIK sets today.

  • Each day of the repeat is a separate task. A missed day does not vanish: its task stays and becomes overdue. When you open the app, missed days are filled in, at most 31 days back.
  • In a shared workspace the due date reminder is carried to the repeat's tasks created after the reminder was set, the same time before their due date. This needs a time on the due date and a connection. The next task is created in advance, so its reminder arrives even if you did not open the app that day. If the next task already exists, set the reminder there too.
  • A repeating task shows the "Repeats" icon.
  • "Stop repeat" is in the task's menu and in the editor. Tasks already created stay. Right after stopping you can tap "Resume".
  • When deleting, choose "Delete only this task" or "Delete this task and all its repeats".

Repeat works both in My LADNIK and in shared workspaces.

Plans

A plan groups several tasks around one goal. A plan has a title, a status (Idea, In progress, Completed), an assignee, an estimated date, a budget and a description.

Link tasks in two ways: from a task via "Linked plan", or from the plan in "Plan tasks" ("Add task", "Link existing task"). Progress reads like "3 of 5 completed". Cancelled tasks are not counted.

A plan is never completed automatically. When every task is done, LADNIK suggests "All tasks are done — complete the plan?". "Complete plan" asks for confirmation if tasks are still open, and those tasks stay open.

Notes and other tools

A note is made of blocks: Text, Checklist and Table. Table columns have types: text, checkbox, number, link, label. Checklist items belong to the note and do not become separate tasks.

There are also Contacts, a Calendar of dated records and Medications with an intake schedule and check-offs.

A medication can have intake reminders, both in a workspace and in My LADNIK. They arrive as notifications on the devices of the person the medication is for. On the device of the person who takes the medication, the intake sheet has "Remind me later": the reminder comes again in 30 minutes.

Finances and currencies

Personal finance is in My LADNIK → "Personal finance", and only you can see it. Workspace finance is added as a tool. You choose its mode when creating it, and it cannot be changed later:

  • "Ordinary finance": workspace accounts with expenses, income, transfers and balances.
  • "Shared expenses": who paid and who owes how much.

Transactions: Expense, Income, Transfer. An expense or income has an amount, account, category, date and comment. A cancelled transaction stays in the history marked "Cancelled", and the balance is recalculated. On your first expense LADNIK offers to create a "Default account".

In shared expenses, turn on "Split between members" and choose "Equally" or "Exact amounts". The overview shows "All expenses", "My share", "I paid" and your balance with each participant. "Settle up" records a payment between participants; the history is in "Settlements".

Without a connection, transactions are saved on the device as "Waiting for network" and sent once you are back online.

Currencies

LADNIK supports 156 ISO 4217 currencies, with popular ones at the top of the list. Each account has its own currency.

  • There are no exchange rates or conversion. Totals in different currencies are shown on separate lines.
  • A transfer is only possible between accounts in the same currency.
  • You choose the default currency when you sign up and change it in Profile → "Personal details" → "Default currency".

Assistant

The Assistant works with your data in the place you choose: a workspace, My LADNIK or "All workspaces". It needs a verified account and a connection.

  • Creates records: a task, plan, note or expense. An expense is always shown for confirmation with its account, category and payer. A task, note or plan can be saved in one tap or adjusted first.
  • Answers questions: which tasks are due today, overdue, coming up or assigned to you, and how much was spent today or this month.
  • By voice: hold the microphone, speak, then release to send. A recording lasts up to 60 seconds; slide left to cancel it.

The text of your request, voice transcripts and limited context are sent to an external AI provider. Voice audio is also sent for transcription. LADNIK does not save the recording or chat history. The remaining limit is shown as a percentage with its reset date. In Profile → "Assistant" you choose the "Character" and "Response length".

Automations

"Automations" open from the Assistant tab (the "Chat / Automations" switch) and from the tool settings of the Tasks and Notes tools. They need an account and a connection, and in a workspace the workspace must be saved to your account. A rule has three parts:

  • "When": a task is created, a task's status changes, or on a schedule.
  • "If": up to five conditions on task fields, such as status or assignee. All of them must match.
  • "Do": create a task or a note, and in My LADNIK also complete a task.

A new rule is saved as a draft on the device. Tap "Submit draft", then "Enable" and confirm. An enabled rule is run by the server, even when the app is closed. The rule acts on your behalf and stops if your access is revoked. What the rule did is shown in "History". In a workspace, rules are set up by the owner and editors.

Telegram

Open Profile → "Integrations" → "Connect Telegram" and start the bot from the link. You need a linked, verified account and a connection. Two things are switched on separately:

  • "Telegram Assistant": add records by message or voice right in the chat.
  • "Telegram notifications": off until you turn them on. You can choose the topics: tasks, mentions, workspaces, access, records, due dates.

"Disconnect Telegram" turns off both the Assistant and the notifications in Telegram. Notifications inside LADNIK stay.

Notifications

The bell on Today opens your notifications. From there you can accept an invitation or claim a task right away. In Profile → "Notifications" you set:

  • Push on this device (needs an account) and Telegram.
  • Notification types: tasks, mentions, workspaces and invitations, roles and access, important changes, deadlines and reminders. Each type has its own "In the app" and Push switches.
  • A mode for each workspace: "Everything important", "Only what concerns me" or "No notifications".
  • "My reminders": "Receive reminders", "Allow repeats (up to 3)" and quiet hours (22:00–08:00 by default). These settings apply to the due date reminders sent to you; changes take effect when you tap "Save". If you turn off "Receive reminders", the reminders already set for you are turned off and do not come back when you turn it on again.

Plans and pricing

Every plan includes all tools. Plans differ in cloud workspaces and participants:

  • Free: 3 cloud workspaces, up to 2 of them shared, up to 3 participants per shared workspace.
  • Pro (personal subscription): 20 cloud workspaces, up to 15 of them shared, up to 5 participants per shared workspace, and an expanded Assistant budget.
  • Team (for the selected team): 25 Team workspaces, 50 members including the owner, and a separate team Assistant budget. Your team membership is in Profile → "Teams". Team does not replace personal Pro and does not grant access to workspaces: people are still invited to each workspace.

Subscribe in Profile → "Subscription" if purchases are available in your version (needs a verified account). The store shows the price before you buy. "Restore purchases" and "Manage subscription" are there too. If you reach a limit, your existing data is kept.

  • ✅ Honest statuses: LADNIK no longer says “saved” when data was not saved, and the Assistant and Finance explain what happened.
  • 📋 Plans: a “Complete plan” action and a hint when every task is done.
  • 💸 Finance: settlements open from the overview, expenses in account history show their category, and a shared expense is a “Split between members” toggle.
  • ↩️ Task, plan and expense editors ask before discarding unsaved changes.
  • 🛡️ Reports: once your report is reviewed, you get a “Your report was reviewed” notification.
  • ✨ One conflict in My LADNIK no longer stops sync for other tools, and medication reminders follow the app language.
